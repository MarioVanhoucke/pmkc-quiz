# PMKC Quiz

**Free online quizzes for the *Integrated Project Management Sourcebook* by Mario Vanhoucke.**

 **Start the quiz: [mariovanhoucke.github.io/pmkc-quiz](https://mariovanhoucke.github.io/pmkc-quiz/)**

## What is it?

The PMKC Quiz lets you test your understanding of the articles in the *Integrated Project Management Sourcebook*. Each quiz belongs to one article from the PM Knowledge Center, and you are asked to read that article before you start.

The questions are grouped into the three parts of the book:

| Part | Topic | Articles | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BS** | Baseline Scheduling | 27 | 137 |
| **RA** | Schedule Risk Analysis | 23 | 124 |
| **PC** | Project Control | 21 | 119 |

Questions are numbered by part (for example BS 12, RA 5 or PC 30), so you can easily find them back in the book.

## How it works

1. Choose a part and an article.
2. Read the PM Knowledge Center article (required reading).
3. Answer the multiple-choice questions. After each answer you see whether it was right, with an explanation and, where relevant, the figure or worked solution from the book.
4. Follow your progress on the **Dashboard**, which shows your scores per part.

## Good to know

- Runs in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, tablets and phones. Nothing to install or download, and no login.
- Your scores are stored only in your own browser. They are not sent anywhere, and they stay available when you come back on the same device and browser. You can reset them on the Dashboard.
